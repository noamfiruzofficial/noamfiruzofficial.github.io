Privacy policy

Firuzville Uploads · Last updated 18 November 2026

Firuzville Uploads is a private tool run by Noam Firuz to upload videos to channels he owns. This policy explains what it does with data from Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) and TikTok.

What we access

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel, and to read that channel's basic details (name and ID) so we can confirm the right channel is connected.
  • We do not access viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts or any other account's data.

How we use it

Only to upload videos the channel owner has approved, with the title and description he chose, and to check that the upload worked.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the macOS Keychain. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone else, and are not stored on any server.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties. Videos go only to the platform they are uploaded to.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions (or the matching settings on Instagram and TikTok). Stored tokens are then deleted from the Keychain.
